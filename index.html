Plan Purchase Protect Prosper

One plan for your home and your money

P360 Partners (Prosperity 360 Partners) brings real estate, insurance and financial planning together, so families don't have to piece their future together from separate advisors who never talk to each other.

For most families, a home is the biggest financial decision they'll ever make. Yet buying a home usually happens apart from the rest of the plan: protecting the family, saving for retirement, and building wealth for the next generation. We believe these decisions belong together, so we look at the whole picture, 360 degrees, before we recommend anything.

Build First. Spend Second. Build a strong foundation first, and let lifestyle grow from there.

Meet Phung Nguyen, MBA, FSCP®

Real Estate Broker · Financial Services Professional · Entrepreneur

MBA, Washington State UniversityFSCP®Series 6 & 63Real estate since 2003English · Tiếng Việt

Born and raised in Vietnam, Phung Nguyen has built a diverse career across the United States and Asia, with more than two decades of experience spanning finance, real estate, business development, market research, and entrepreneurship.

Her journey has been shaped by a passion for business, a curiosity about the world, and a belief that financial success is about more than money. It is about creating opportunities, building lasting security, and having the freedom to live life on your own terms.

From financial analyst to entrepreneur

After earning her MBA from Washington State University, Phung began her professional career in Los Angeles as a systems and financial analyst. While working full time, she obtained her California real estate license in 2003, marking the beginning of a real estate career that continues today.

In 2005, she returned to Washington State and established her own real estate and mortgage brokerage businesses. As a licensed Managing Broker and Mortgage Broker, she wanted to offer clients a more complete experience, from finding the right property to securing financing. This entrepreneurial chapter laid the foundation for her long-term interest in real estate investing and helping families build wealth through property ownership.

An international career and a global perspective

Phung's career eventually took her and her husband on an overseas adventure as expatriates. They began their journey in Hong Kong, where they lived for more than a year, before establishing their home base in Vietnam. Her husband's work with Black & Veatch involved projects across the country, giving their family the opportunity to live and experience life extensively in Northern, Central, and Southern Vietnam.

During this time, Phung continued building her corporate career. She joined Nielsen, a global market research company, as a Senior Manager, working with major fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) brands seeking to understand consumers and grow their market share in Southeast Asia. She later joined Philip Morris International, where she was responsible for marketing research covering Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia.

These experiences gave her a firsthand understanding of international business, consumer behavior, market dynamics, and the importance of adapting to different cultures and economic environments. More importantly, living and working across different countries helped shape her perspective on opportunity, risk, and the many ways people define success.

Returning to the United States and real estate investing

Toward the end of 2018, Phung and her family returned to the United States to be closer to family. She subsequently worked with Boeing as a business development consultant before relocating to Florida in 2021. Following the move, Phung shifted much of her attention toward managing and expanding her family's real estate investments while raising her two sons.

Throughout her professional transitions, real estate has remained a constant part of her life since 2003. Her experience includes residential and commercial real estate, investment properties, and farmland. She has maintained her involvement in the industry through brokerage, property ownership, and real estate investing, eventually obtaining her Florida real estate broker license.

Having worked in real estate across California, Washington, and Florida, she brings both professional experience and the perspective of an investor who understands the opportunities and responsibilities that come with owning property.

Expanding into financial services

As her two sons grew older and became more independent, Phung decided to return to her professional interests with a renewed sense of purpose. In 2026, she joined New York Life, where she expanded her knowledge of life insurance, retirement planning, and financial protection strategies. During her time there, she obtained her Series 6 and Series 63 securities licenses and earned the Financial Services Certified Professional (FSCP®) designation from The American College of Financial Services. She is also continuing her professional education toward the Chartered Financial Consultant (ChFC®) and CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER® (CFP®) credentials.

Although she valued her experience, training, and relationships at New York Life, Phung realized that her long-term vision extended beyond traditional insurance and financial services. Toward the end of 2026, she made the decision to pursue an independent business model, allowing her to bring together the different areas of expertise she had developed throughout her career.

Today, Phung is affiliated with Vantage Financial Alliance and is building her own financial services agency, with the goal of providing families access to a broader range of financial solutions and professional resources. Her vision is to connect financial protection, retirement planning, real estate, investing, mortgage services, and tax-related resources through a network of appropriately licensed professionals and strategic partners. She also hopes to mentor and develop other professionals who share her entrepreneurial spirit and want to build meaningful careers in financial services.

Family, freedom, and the meaning of wealth

Beyond her professional life, Phung is a wife, a mother of two sons, an avid traveler, and someone who genuinely enjoys exploring new places and cultures. Having lived and worked in different parts of the world, she values the experiences, relationships, and perspectives that come from stepping outside one's comfort zone.

For Phung, the goal is not simply to offer individual financial products, but to help families understand how their financial decisions fit together.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what families ask us most. Don't see your question? Call or text 206-636-5354.

What is P360 Partners?

P360 Partners is the team name used by Phung Nguyen. We help families with real estate, protection and long-term financial planning, so a home, insurance and retirement plan can work together. Real estate services are offered through Florida Realty Investments IV. Insurance and financial services are offered through Vantage Financial Alliance.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. The first chat is free and comes with no obligation to buy anything. We use it to understand where you are and what you want to work on.

Can we talk in Vietnamese?

Yes. We work with families in English and Vietnamese. Many families find it easier to talk about money in the language they're most comfortable with.

Are your real estate and insurance services the same company?

No. Florida Realty Investments IV and Vantage Financial Alliance are separately owned and operated companies and are not affiliated with each other. Phung Nguyen works with each one separately, under its own license.

Which states do you serve?

Real estate services are offered in Florida through Florida Realty Investments IV. Insurance and financial services are currently available in California, Florida, North Carolina, Tennessee, Texas and Washington, with more states coming. Ask us about your state.

What should I bring to the first chat?

Just your questions and goals. You don't need any documents for the first conversation. If it makes sense to go further, we'll tell you what would be helpful to gather.

Do you give tax or legal advice?

No. When you need tax, legal or mortgage help, we can connect you with separately licensed professionals. A referral is not an endorsement.

Do I need experience to join the team?

No experience is needed. A license is required, and we help you through the licensing process. Many people start part-time while keeping their current job. This is an independent contractor opportunity, and income depends on individual effort and results; no earnings are guaranteed.
